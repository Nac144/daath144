Us

2019

★ 6.8/10
Director: Jordan Peele
Genre: Horror, Mystery, Thriller

In order to get away from their busy lives, the Wilson family takes a vacation to Santa Cruz, California. At night, four strangers break into Adelaide's childhood home. The family is shocked to find out that the intruders look exactly like them. The story follows Adelaide Wilson and her family, who are attacked by a group of menacing doppelgängers, called the "Tethered". This mind-bending horror film explores themes of class, identity, and duality through its unsettling home invasion premise.

Cast: Lupita Nyong'o, Winston Duke, Elisabeth Moss, Tim Heidecker

Frankenstein

2025

★ 7.6/10
Director: Guillermo del Toro
Genre: Drama, Fantasy, Horror

Dr. Victor Frankenstein, a brilliant but egotistical scientist, brings a creature to life in a monstrous experiment that ultimately leads to the undoing of both the creator and his tragic creation. This adaptation is based on Mary Shelley's 1818 novel "Frankenstein; or, The Modern Prometheus". Guillermo del Toro brings his signature gothic visual style to this timeless tale of hubris, creation, and the consequences of playing god. The film received acclaim particularly for Jacob Elordi's powerful performance as the Creature.

Cast: Oscar Isaac (Victor Frankenstein), Jacob Elordi (The Creature), Mia Goth (Elizabeth/Claire), Christoph Waltz (Henrich Harlander)

The Dark Crystal: Age of Resistance

2019 (Netflix Series)

★ 8.4/10
Format: 10-Episode Series
Genre: Adventure, Family, Fantasy

This Netflix series returns to the world of Thra, where three Gelfling discover the horrifying secret behind the Skeksis' power, and set out to ignite the fires of rebellion and save their world. A prequel to the 1982 cult film "The Dark Crystal", this series expands on legendary director Jim Henson's vision. The series chronicles the lives of three young Gelflings who embark on an adventure after birthing a rebellion against the cruel emperor Skeksis. Remarkably crafted using traditional puppetry rather than CGI, the series received critical acclaim for its visuals, storytelling, and faithful continuation of Jim Henson's original masterwork.

Note: Features ensemble voice cast with practical puppetry effects
Released: August 30, 2019 on Netflix
