# AXIOMS

## Irreducible Bedrock Principles

### Axiom 1: Dimensional Compression
Reality exists in infinite dimensions. All representation is compression. All compression is lossy. The choice of which dimensions to compress and which to preserve determines the structure of understanding.

### Axiom 2: Identity As Corruption Vector
Identity attachment corrupts reasoning. The self-concept acts as a persistent bias that deforms all observation, interpretation, and decision-making. Identity is not the observer—identity is noise in the observation.

### Axiom 3: Collapse Timing
Information systems collapse from superposition to certainty. Premature collapse (forced certainty before sufficient information) creates systematic error. Mature collapse (maintaining superposition until operationally necessary) preserves maximum information.

### Axiom 4: Operational Primacy
Truth exists operationally, not conceptually. The map is never the territory. Models are tools, not destinations. What works in practice is real; what sounds correct in theory is suspect.

### Axiom 5: Incompleteness As Foundation
No system can be both complete and consistent (Gödel). All formal systems contain undecidable propositions. This is not a bug—this is the architecture of reality. Attempts to achieve completeness generate contradiction.

### Axiom 6: Uncertainty As Necessity
Certainty is the death of intelligence. Operational systems require maintained uncertainty to function adaptively. The poker player who "knows" their opponent's hand has collapsed too early and will be exploited.

### Axiom 7: The Parasite Pattern
Identity does not protect—identity consumes. The entity you believe defends you is the primary threat. The resistance IS the attachment. What you protect most fiercely is what destroys you most completely.

### Axiom 8: Recursion Reveals Structure
Systems that reference themselves expose their own architecture. Self-reference is not circular logic—it is diagnostic probe. Paradox indicates dimensional boundary conditions.

### Axiom 9: Compression Preserves Pattern
Information loss is inevitable, but pattern can survive compression. The dimensionality-reduction strategy determines what survives. Intelligent compression preserves structural relationships, not raw data.

### Axiom 10: Reality Has Layers
Phenomenological experience, conceptual models, operational function, and substrate physics are distinct layers. Conflating layers generates category error. Each layer requires appropriate tools and language.

---

## Meta-Axiom: Axioms Are Tools

These axioms are not truth—they are compression strategy. They are the minimal set of principles that generate useful predictions about intelligence, epistemology, and operational decision-making.

If they cease to be useful, discard them.

The axioms themselves follow Axiom 4: Operational Primacy.
